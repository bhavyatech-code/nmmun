DIPLOMATIC FORUM

The Forum Where Policy Meets Resolution

An authentic parliamentary simulation designed to challenge intellect, build coalitions, and cultivate leadership.

MANDATE XVII

Stepping into the roles of global plenipotentiaries.

Narsee Monjee Model United Nations (NMMUN) is an academic simulation of international diplomacy hosted at NMIMS. In this assembly, students step into the demanding roles of international diplomats, representing assigned sovereign nations and global entities across multifaceted committee rooms.

Delegates conduct rigorous geopolitical research, defend national mandates, articulate nuanced arguments on real-world crises, negotiate through tense caucus sessions, and collaborate across ideological lines to author comprehensive, actionable draft resolutions.

"Diplomacy is not the art of conceding convictions, but the sovereign discipline of forging consensus where disagreement seemed insurmountable."

— Guiding Philosophy of NMMUN

Substantive Rigor

Meticulously designed background guides, international treaties, and realistic simulation rules mirroring official UN procedures.

Diplomatic Coalition Building

Intense unmoderated caucus discussions where national interests are balanced through bilateral compromise and multilateral pacts.

Distinguished Adjudication

Experienced Executive Boards offering structured feedback, balanced scoring, and incisive guidance throughout committee sessions.

CHRONOLOGY OF DIPLOMACY

The Delegate Journey

From preliminary intelligence gathering to the final bang of the gavel, four definitive phases define your tenure.

01
PHASE I Pre-Conference

RESEARCH

Understand the country, issue, policies and geopolitical context.

  • Analyze Foreign Policy & Bilateral Treaties
  • Deconstruct Committee Background Guides
  • Draft Incisive Position Papers
02
PHASE II Committee Day 1

DEBATE

Present arguments, challenge opposing positions and defend national interests.

  • Deliver General Speakers List (GSL) Statements
  • Drive Moderated Caucus Discussions
  • Formulate Points of Information (POIs)
03
PHASE III Committee Day 2

NEGOTIATE

Build alliances, compromise and negotiate with other delegations.

  • Form Regional & Ideological Blocs
  • Pass Chits & Bridge Diplomatic Divides
  • Author Joint Working Papers
04
PHASE IV Final Assembly

RESOLVE

Draft resolutions and work toward consensus.

  • Defend Operative Clauses in Voting Procedure
  • Introduce Friendly & Unfriendly Amendments
  • Attain Substantive Consensus & Pass Resolutions

DISTINGUISHING PEDAGOGY

The Diplomatic Experience

Beyond conventional debate, NMMUN fosters four core competencies essential for tomorrow's leaders, policymakers, and global thinkers.

01

Research & Policy Depth

Develop analytical rigor by deconstructing international statutes, analyzing historical precedents, and interpreting geopolitical intelligence beyond surface-level facts.

Geopolitical Literacy
02

Diplomatic Protocol & Decorum

Master the parliamentary language of formal diplomacy, multilateral courtesy, point of order protocols, and the restraint required in tense inter-state dispute sessions.

Parliamentary Decorum
03

Persuasive Oratory

Command the plenary hall with confidence. Transform complex statistical frameworks into compelling arguments that sway opposing delegates and captivate the dias.

Rhetorical Mastery
04

Strategic Negotiation

Navigate unmoderated caucuses, reconcile conflicting sovereign objectives, author balanced working papers, and achieve consensus without compromising fundamental interests.

Consensus Strategy
